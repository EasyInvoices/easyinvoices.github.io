Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how Easy Invoice by Zareen at easyinvoices.github.io handles information when you create invoices, quotations and measurement reports, use Google sign-in, or visit the website.

Your choice of storage matters. Guest records are stored in your browser. If you sign in with Google, the invoice tool uses Firebase to store and synchronise your company profile, clients and saved documents. Hosting, sign-in and advertising providers may also receive technical information when their services load.

1. Information you enter

The tool processes the details you choose to provide, including business names, logos, contact details, addresses, tax identifiers, bank details shown on documents, client information, invoice and quotation items, amounts, notes and measurements. These details are used to create, save, edit and export your documents.

Only enter information you are entitled to use, including information about your clients. A bank detail entered for display on an invoice is document content; the invoice tool does not use it to initiate a bank payment.

2. Guest mode and browser storage

In guest mode, the invoice tool saves records and a browser identifier in local storage on your device. Session storage remembers your access choice. The measurement tool saves its draft in local browser storage and reads locally saved business and client details.

Guest records are not automatically uploaded through the invoice tool's cloud-sync feature while you are signed out. Other services described below may still load and receive technical information. People with access to the same browser profile may be able to access locally saved records. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove or prevent the retention of these records.

3. Google sign-in and cloud storage

Google sign-in is optional. Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture to authenticate you and display your account. Your Google password is handled by Google, rather than entered into this invoice tool.

When you are signed in, your company profile, clients and saved invoices and quotations are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account identifier, along with an update time. The app loads these records on sign-in and synchronises changes. It may offer to copy existing guest records into a new cloud account. The measurement draft remains a separate local-browser record.

Local copies can remain on a device after sign-out. Signing out or clearing browser data does not by itself delete cloud records. Google processes service and authentication information under its Privacy Policy; see also Firebase privacy information.

Service activity and administrator access

To manage usage and access, Firebase assigns guest browsers an anonymous identifier. We record Google account or guest status, first and last activity times, session counts, counts of saved documents and clients, and counts of export attempts and measurement entries. Google users’ name and email accompany these summaries. Counts are approximate and may be delayed. Guest invoice and client contents remain in browser storage; only these activity summaries are sent to Firebase.

The site administrator, craftsbyzareen@gmail.com, can review these summaries and signed-in users’ saved cloud business profiles, invoices, quotations and client records for support, abuse prevention and service management. The administrator can block access or set account limits. This access is restricted through Firebase security rules. Activity summaries and access settings remain until removed by the operator; contact us to request access or deletion. Guest identifiers can change if browser data is cleared.

When you use the invoice or measurement tool, your browser may contact ipapi.is to estimate the city, region and country associated with your internet connection. That provider receives your IP address and technical request information; we do not send it your Google email, name, invoices or client records. We store only the approximate city, region, country and lookup time with your activity summary in Firebase. The site does not save the returned IP address or coordinates in that summary, and does not request your device's GPS location.

The estimate is cached in your browser for up to 24 hours to reduce requests. It may describe a VPN, shared connection or network gateway rather than your physical location. Failed or blocked lookups appear as unavailable. Location summaries are available to the administrator for understanding service usage and remain with activity data until removed by the operator; the access and deletion contact below also applies to these summaries.

4. Google advertising and cookies

The site includes Google AdSense code and may display advertisements when enabled. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies or similar identifiers to select ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalise advertising across websites.

Advertising services may process technical and usage information such as your IP address, browser or device information, identifiers and interactions with ads. Read how Google uses information from partner sites.

You can manage Google's personalised advertising through My Ad Center and find opt-out choices for participating third-party advertising companies at YourAdChoices. Browser settings can also restrict cookies. These choices may change ad personalisation without removing all ads. This policy explains data use; viewing it is not consent to advertising cookies.

5. Hosting and other service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the website. The app also loads software, styles or fonts from Google/Firebase, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare's cdnjs, jsDelivr and unpkg. Requests to these providers can disclose technical information, including an IP address, browser information and the requested resource, and may include referral information sent by your browser.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement for hosting information. Providers may process information on infrastructure in other countries, subject to their own policies and service terms.

6. Downloads, sharing and support

PDFs, images and backup files contain the information you choose to include. Once you download or share a file, its storage and recipients are controlled by you and by any app you use to share it. Protect backups and exported documents, particularly when they contain client or financial information.

If you contact the site operator by email, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or full invoice backups unless they are needed and a suitable way to provide them has been agreed.

7. Retention, access and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them through the tool, clear this site's browser data, or your browser removes them. Use the available document and client controls to review, change or delete records. Guest mode provides a Data Import / Export option for backups.

For a signed-in account, record changes and deletions are sent to cloud storage when synchronisation succeeds. Clearing a local measurement draft does not delete invoices or cloud data. Copies you have exported or shared are not removed by deleting a record in the tool.

To request access to or deletion of your remaining cloud account data, contact the address below from the email associated with your account. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account. Service-provider logs and backups are subject to the providers' retention processes. Privacy rights may vary with your location.

8. Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Easy Invoice by Zareen site operator at craftsbyzareen@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
